Delete your Restrainify account

This page explains how to delete your Restrainify account and the data stored with it. Restrainify is published by [[LEGAL NAME]].

Last updated: [[DATE]]

1. Delete your account inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Restrainify and tap the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Delete account. (It is shown when you are signed in.)
  3. Read the notice, type DELETE in the box, then tap Permanently delete account.
  4. Confirm in the pop-up by tapping Delete Account.
  5. When the app shows Account Deleted, your account is gone and you are signed out. If the app shows an error instead, your account was not deleted. Check your connection and try again.

Your account and the data listed below are removed from our servers.

2. Can't open the app? Ask us to delete it

Email [[DELETION REQUEST EMAIL]] from the email address of the Google account you used to sign in to Restrainify, with the subject "Delete my Restrainify account".

We will confirm that the request came from the account owner and delete the account within [[NUMBER]] days. We will email you when it is done.

3. What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete the data tied to it:

  • Your account profile: sign-in ID, email address, name and profile-picture link.
  • Your account settings stored on our servers.
  • Recovery data you synced: urge, relapse and Burst entries and any notes you wrote.
  • Tracker entries you synced, if you used the optional tracker.
  • Website rules (allowed or blocked sites) you added and synced.
  • Your coin balance and daily claims.
  • Your sign-in sessions and the random device ID the app created.

5. Data that stays on your phone

Some data is stored only on your device and is not on our servers: your local database (streaks, logs, settings and app rules), and your permission settings. Deleting your account does not erase it. To remove it, use Settings → Delete local data in the app, or uninstall Restrainify.

Restrainify does not keep screen images, a record of the websites you visit, or your app-usage history on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.
