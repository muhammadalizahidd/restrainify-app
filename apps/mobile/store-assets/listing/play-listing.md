Protection runs on your phone. No account is needed, and Restrainify keeps no browsing history.

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
Restrainify asks only for what each feature needs, and explains each one before you turn it on.
• Accessibility service: detects which app and screen is open so Restrainify can block apps, Reels and Shorts, enforce limits and schedules, show the NSFW filter and protect its own settings during an uninstall lock. Restrainify does not use it to collect or share personal data.
• VPN service: creates a local filter on your phone that blocks adult websites by name. Your other traffic does not pass through it, and no browsing history is kept.
• Usage access: measures how long each app is open, for app limits and screen time stats.
• Device administrator: used only while you have uninstall protection on, and released when the lock ends.

Restrainify helps you build self control. It is not tamper-proof, and works best together with your own commitment.
